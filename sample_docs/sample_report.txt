ACME CORPORATION — Q3 2024 FINANCIAL REPORT

Executive Summary
-----------------
Acme Corporation delivered strong performance in Q3 2024, with total revenue reaching $48.7 million,
representing a 14.2% year-over-year increase. Operating income improved to $9.3 million, driven by
efficiency gains in the manufacturing division and expansion into three new regional markets.

Revenue Breakdown
-----------------
Product Sales:       $31.2 million  (+18% YoY)
Service Revenue:     $12.4 million  (+7% YoY)
Licensing & IP:       $5.1 million  (+9% YoY)
Total Revenue:       $48.7 million  (+14.2% YoY)

Operating Expenses
------------------
Cost of Goods Sold:  $22.1 million
Research & Dev:       $6.8 million
Sales & Marketing:    $5.3 million
General & Admin:      $5.2 million
Total OpEx:          $39.4 million

Operating Income:     $9.3 million (19.1% margin)
Net Income:           $7.1 million (14.6% margin)

Headcount & Operations
-----------------------
Total Employees: 1,240 (up from 1,105 at end of Q2 2024)
New Hires Q3:    162
Attrition Q3:    27

Key Milestones
--------------
- Launched AcmePro v4.0 in August 2024, now deployed at 340 enterprise clients.
- Opened new regional offices in Austin, TX and Amsterdam, Netherlands.
- Completed acquisition of DataBridge Inc. for $8.2 million in cash.
- Awarded ISO 27001 security certification for cloud infrastructure.

Outlook for Q4 2024
--------------------
Management expects revenue in the range of $51–54 million for Q4, supported by the holiday
product cycle and the full-quarter contribution of DataBridge revenues. Capital expenditure
guidance remains at $3.5 million for infrastructure upgrades.

Risk Factors
------------
- Supply chain disruptions in Asia-Pacific may impact hardware delivery timelines.
- Macroeconomic headwinds could slow enterprise software procurement in Europe.
- Integration costs from the DataBridge acquisition are expected to reduce Q4 margins by 1–2%.
