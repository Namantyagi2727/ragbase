GLOBALTECH INC — EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY
Version 3.1 | Effective January 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE
----------
This policy outlines the types of leave available to GlobalTech employees, eligibility criteria,
accrual rules, and the process for requesting and approving leave.

2. SCOPE
--------
This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of GlobalTech Inc. and its wholly
owned subsidiaries. Contractors and consultants are not covered under this policy.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE (VACATION)
---------------------------
Full-time employees accrue annual leave as follows:
  - Years 0–2:    15 days per year  (1.25 days/month)
  - Years 3–5:    18 days per year  (1.5 days/month)
  - Years 6–10:   22 days per year  (~1.83 days/month)
  - Years 10+:    25 days per year  (~2.08 days/month)

Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rated basis proportional to their contracted hours.

Maximum carry-over: Employees may carry over up to 5 unused days into the next calendar year.
Any days above this cap are forfeited on December 31.

4. SICK LEAVE
--------------
All employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave does not roll over.
For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate from a licensed practitioner
is required. Extended sick leave beyond 10 days may be covered under short-term disability.

5. PARENTAL LEAVE
------------------
Primary caregiver: 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.
Employees must give at least 6 weeks' notice before planned parental leave.

6. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
---------------------
Immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling): 5 paid days.
Extended family (grandparent, in-law, aunt/uncle): 3 paid days.
Close friend (at manager's discretion): 1 paid day.

7. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
-------------------
GlobalTech observes 11 federal public holidays per year. Employees required to work on a public
holiday will receive either a day off in lieu or time-and-a-half pay, at the manager's discretion.

8. UNPAID LEAVE
----------------
Employees may request up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons, subject to
manager approval and business needs. Unpaid leave does not affect service continuity calculations.

9. LEAVE REQUEST PROCESS
-------------------------
1. Submit a leave request via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave.
2. Your direct manager approves or declines within 3 business days.
3. HR records the approved leave and updates your leave balance.
4. For emergency or unplanned leave, notify your manager as early as possible on the day of absence.

10. CONTACT
-----------
For questions about this policy, contact the People & Culture team at hr@globaltech-example.com
or reach out to your HR Business Partner.
